Privacy Policy

Wafai Homes — Mohammad Wafai, Realtor® with eXp Realty · CA DRE# 02203253
Last updated: November 15, 2026

What we collect

When you use a form on this website — the free guide booklet download, the free home evaluation, or the consultation booking form — we collect the information you type in: your name, email address, phone number, the city or property address you are asking about, your buying or selling timeline, and any message you add. We do not collect payment information on this website, and there are no user accounts.

How we use it

We use your information only to respond to your request: to send you the guide booklets you asked for, prepare your free home evaluation, schedule your consultation, and follow up with personalized real estate help related to your request. Mohammad Wafai may contact you by phone, text, or email about your request.

What we never do

We never sell your personal information. We do not share it with third parties for their own marketing. Information is shared only as needed to serve you — for example with eXp Realty as Mohammad's brokerage, or with service providers who help operate this website — or when required by law.

Guide downloads

The free Buyer's and Seller's Guide Booklets are delivered as downloads hosted on Google Drive. When you open a download link, Google's own privacy policy applies to that visit.

Cookies and analytics

This website is a simple static site. It does not run advertising trackers. The hosting provider (Cloudflare) may process standard technical logs such as IP address and browser type to deliver and secure the site.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to stop contacting you, or to correct or delete the information you shared, by emailing wafaihomes@gmail.com or calling (530) 301-6691. Replying STOP to a text message opts you out of texts.

California privacy rights

California residents may request to know what personal information we hold about them, and may request its deletion, using the contact details above. We do not sell personal information, so there is no sale to opt out of.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
